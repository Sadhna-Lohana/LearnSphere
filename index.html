GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about LearnSphere.

What is LearnSphere?

LearnSphere is an online learning platform concept where learners can explore different subjects and discover new skills.

Are the courses free?

The courses displayed in this portfolio demo are labelled free. Real lessons and course enrollment are not connected yet.

Can beginners use LearnSphere?

Yes! The sample course collection focuses on beginner-friendly topics such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript and graphic design.

Do I need to attend classes at a fixed time?

The sample courses are presented as self-paced learning. A real platform would need actual course materials to support this.

Will I receive a certificate?

Certificate generation is not currently available. It could be added as a future feature of LearnSphere.
